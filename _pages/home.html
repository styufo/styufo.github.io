Tianyi Song

I am a PhD researcher in Robotics and Computation at the UCL Hawkes Institute, advised by Associate Professor Francisco Vasconcelos. My research focuses on computer vision and surgical robotics, particularly deformable SLAM, online reconstruction, and Gaussian Splatting.

I received my M.Sc. from Southeast University in 2024 and my B.Sc. in Communication Engineering from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. My earlier work explored multimodal image generation and image restoration.
