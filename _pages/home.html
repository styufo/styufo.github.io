PhD Researcher · UCL Hawkes Institute

Tianyi Song

Building vision systems that see, track, and reconstruct deformable surgical scenes.

I am a PhD researcher in Robotics and Computation at University College London, advised by Francisco Vasconcelos. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, surgical robotics, and 3D scene understanding.

Research focus

Reliable perception for surgery

I develop geometry-aware learning systems that remain useful when tissue deforms, instruments occlude the view, and camera poses are missing or noisy.

01

Surgical scene understanding

Recovering anatomy and motion from challenging endoscopic video.

02

Deformable SLAM

Jointly estimating camera motion and non-rigid scene dynamics online.

03

3D reconstruction

Building accurate, view-consistent anatomy with Gaussian Splatting.

Deformable SLAM · Online reconstruction

Track2Map: Online Deformable SLAM with Motion-Aware Pose Optimization in Robotic Surgery

Tianyi Song, Sierra Bonilla, Xinwei Ju, Evangelos Mazomenos, Danail Stoyanov, Adam Schmidt, Omid Mohareri, Sophia Bano, Francisco Vasconcelos

IEEE RA-L 2026

Occlusion removal · 2D Gaussian Splatting

Diff2DGS: Reliable Reconstruction of Occluded Surgical Scenes via 2D Gaussian Splatting

Tianyi Song, Danail Stoyanov, Evangelos Mazomenos, Francisco Vasconcelos

Combines temporally consistent video inpainting with deformable 2D Gaussian Splatting to recover appearance and geometry behind surgical instruments.
