Assessment Methods

Evaluation focused on generalization, not leaderboard gaming

The current iMED proposal uses conventional but clinically meaningful metrics: geometric accuracy for pose estimation and photometric fidelity for deformable novel view synthesis, all computed on hidden test scenes.

Primary ranking metrics

iMED PE

Primary ranking metric: Absolute Trajectory Error (ATE)

Also reported: relative rotational error (ROT), registered tracking fraction (RTF), runtime transparency

iMED NVS

Primary ranking metric: PSNR

Also reported: SSIM, training FPS, rendering FPS

Ranking procedure

Task Per-sequence computation Aggregate rank Fallback for missing outputs
iMED PE ATE, ROT, and valid-frame fraction computed sequence-by-sequence Mean ATE over all hidden test sequences Missing outputs count as failure cases with capped error and zero tracking fraction
iMED NVS PSNR and SSIM computed per frame, then averaged per sequence Mean PSNR over all hidden test sequences Missing outputs contribute zero-quality frames in the aggregate

Statistical analysis

The working draft proposes hierarchical bootstrap confidence intervals with 1000 iterations, resampling test sequences and then frames within sequences. This is intended to reflect the nested structure of the evaluation data and to report both score and rank variability.

Practical note. For methods that require scene-specific training at evaluation time, the proposal states that runtime limits will be enforced for practicality. Those exact caps should be written explicitly once the challenge infrastructure is finalized.

Validation and hidden test philosophy

  • Participants are expected to self-evaluate on public development data using official scripts.
  • Intermediate validation submissions are planned before the final test phase.
  • Final rankings are computed only on hidden test scenes that remain unseen to participants.

Prizes and awards

Current proposal

For each task, the proposal states that there will be monetary prizes for first, second, and third place. Final amounts are still to be determined.

Publication policy

The proposal indicates that participating teams with valid submissions will be eligible for inclusion in a joint challenge paper, with up to three named contributors per team depending on total participation. Teams are expected to submit a structured methods report and may later publish separate papers that cite the official challenge publication.
